Free instrument recordings

Music Coding includes 53 original recordings (53.0 MB) from Versilian Studios and Karoryfer Samples. Thank you to these creators and their contributors.

These recordings are dedicated to the public under CC0 1.0. You can use and share them in music and apps, including commercial projects.

How this small selection works

Each pitched instrument has several recorded notes. The app changes playback speed for notes between those recordings. Notes far outside the recorded range can sound less natural. Each note uses one playing strength and one take. Bowed strings play finite recordings and do not loop forever.

The two drum kits combine seven separate drum recordings. Each kit uses a different concert bass drum and snare. Play C4 for a drum recording at its original speed; this does not mean the drum is tuned to C.
