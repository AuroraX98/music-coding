Free instrument recordings

Music Coding includes 26 recordings from the Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL), created by Versilian Studios LLC and contributors.

These recordings are dedicated to the public under CC0 1.0. You can use and share them in music and apps, including commercial projects. Thank you to the creators for making them available.

Included instruments

  • Soft grand piano: A gentle recorded piano. Try it for chords and melodies.
  • Folk harp: Plucked strings with a clear ringing sound. Try notes one after another.
  • Marimba: Wooden bars hit with mallets. Try a gentle repeating tune.
  • Vibraphone: Soft mallets tap metal bars for a warm ringing sound. Try slow chords.
  • Glockenspiel: Metal bars with a bright bell sound. Try high sparkling notes.
  • Short recorder: A breathy wooden flute sound played in short bursts. Long notes will still sound short.
  • Short tenor sax: A rich reed sound played in short bursts. Long notes will still sound short.

This small starter collection is about 22 MB. It uses several recorded pitches for each instrument and changes playback speed for notes between them. Very low or high notes can sound less natural. Recorder and saxophone recordings are short notes.
