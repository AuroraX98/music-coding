All WAVs retain their original bytes. Source paths, URLs, hashes, sizes and scientific root notes are recorded in manifest.json. This is a small selection. Notes between roots are pitch shifted. One playing strength and take per note; no automatic sample looping.

The creator confirms the free collections are now CC0 at https://shop.karoryfer.com/pages/free-samples (checked 2026-10-14), including older downloads with earlier terms. Original README and current repository CC0 license are preserved. Creator statement: "All our free sample libraries are under a Creative Commons Zero license."

Growlybass: Squier Jazz Bass, edited and mapped by Karoryfer Samples / D. Smolken. Recordings sound one octave below their filename and SFZ key-center notation; frequency checks set roots to actual sounding scientific pitch.

Sample licenses remain separate from app and dependency licenses. Instrument names identify recordings; no endorsement is claimed.
