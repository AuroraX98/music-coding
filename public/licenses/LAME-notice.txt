LAME project and source: https://lame.sourceforge.io/
LAME licensing explanation: https://lame.sourceforge.io/license.txt
LAME source archives: https://sourceforge.net/projects/lame/files/lame/
Encoder wrapper source and WebAssembly build instructions:
https://github.com/arseneyr/wasm-media-encoders/tree/master
https://github.com/arseneyr/wasm-media-encoders/blob/master/Makefile

The wrapper builds libmp3lame with --disable-decoder and --disable-frontend.
The app loads the MP3 WebAssembly module as a separate file. It can be replaced
by a compatible rebuilt encoder module using the upstream build instructions.
The app makes no changes to the encoder library.
